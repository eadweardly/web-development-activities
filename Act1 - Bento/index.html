poem

What the heart keeps

I still set a place for you
in the quiet hours of the day,
the stairwell, the long way home,
the minute before the light comes in.

You are not here. I know.
But longing is a kind of faith
that something loved this much
cannot simply stop being.

And if the ache is all that's left,
let it stay.
It means someone was worth missing.

Longing lives between memory and hope. We look back at who we were with someone, and forward to a future that may never arrive, while the present is just the place where we wait.
