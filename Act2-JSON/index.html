Why EMPTY PASSENGER?

The engines still hum. The leather still smells like summer. Only the seat is empty.

Every car in this collection was built to carry two people somewhere. These six still run, still shine, and still turn heads — but the passenger side stays empty.

EMPTY PASSENGER is a personal collection of timeless cars, kept the way you keep a memory: polished, protected, and never quite let go. It is a small tribute to the people who once sat beside us, and to the roads we can still drive but can no longer share.
